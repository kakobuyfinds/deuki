How to use the spreadsheet

  1. Open the source listing

    Match the item ID, color and size. If the listing provides a merchant URL, check whether your shopping agent accepts it before paying.

  2. Compare measurements

    For tops, compare the chest width and body length with a garment you own. For shoes, check what the seller’s size chart measures.

  3. Estimate the delivered price

    Add the item price, domestic shipping, agent fees and estimated international shipping. Check taxes or duties applicable to your destination.

  4. Review warehouse photos

    If your agent offers quality-control photos, check the color, size label and visible condition. Ask for measurements if the fit is unclear.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order these items through SuperBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for the original merchant URL. Check with SuperBuy whether that listing and variant can be purchased and shipped to your destination.

Why can the USD price change?

The catalog shows an approximate USD conversion of the listed CNY price. The final amount can vary with the selected option, exchange rate, shipping and fees.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Search by item ID on HipobuyFind or reopen the relevant category. When comparing a replacement, verify the seller, size chart and available options before placing an order.
